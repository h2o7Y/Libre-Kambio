## Official exchange-rate and monetary sources

The application reads public information from official institutions, including:

- European Central Bank (ECB)
- Bank of Russia (CBR)
- Central Bank of the UAE (AED peg)
- Saudi Central Bank / SAMA (SAR peg)
- Qatar Central Bank (QAR peg)
- Central Bank of Bahrain (BHD parity)
- Central Bank of Oman (OMR parity)
- Banco Central de la República Argentina / BCRA (ARS)
- Banco Central de Chile / BCCh (CLP)
- Banco de la República, Colombia (COP)
- Banco Central del Paraguay / BCP (PYG)
- Banco Central de Reserva del Perú / BCRP (PEN)
- Banco Central del Uruguay / BCU (UYU)
- Banco Central de Bolivia / BCB (optional indicative cross-check)

These institutions do not sponsor, endorse, certify, guarantee or maintain Libre Kambio. Their names identify the public data sources used by the application.
